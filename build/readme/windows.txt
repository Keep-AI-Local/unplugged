UNPLUGGED: AI that lives entirely on your computer
Free · No account · No internet needed · keepailocal.org

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. If you haven't already: right-click the downloaded "Unplugged-Windows-x64.zip" and choose
   "Extract All...", then "Extract". (It won't work from inside the zip.)
2. Open the extracted "Unplugged" folder and double-click "Unplugged" (Unplugged.exe).

WINDOWS WILL PROBABLY WARN YOU. HERE'S WHY, AND WHAT TO DO.
You'll likely see a blue box: "Windows protected your PC".
  -> Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".

This happens because Unplugged isn't "code-signed". Signing costs hundreds of dollars a year, and
Unplugged is a free project with nothing to sell. The warning means "Microsoft doesn't know who made
this", not "this is dangerous". If you'd like to check it yourself:
  - Every line of the code is public: github.com/Keep-AI-Local/unplugged
  - Each download's fingerprint (SHA-256) is listed on the GitHub release page.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  Unplugged.exe ........ the app
  models\ .............. the AI itself: one file (Google's Gemma 3 1B), plus its license files
Nothing is installed, nothing is written to your computer, and nothing you type ever leaves it.
To remove Unplugged, just delete the folder.

Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use found at ai.google.dev/gemma/terms
(see models\NOTICE.txt). Unplugged's own code is MIT-licensed (LICENSE.txt).
