UNPLUGGED: AI that lives entirely on your computer
Free · No account · No internet needed · keepailocal.org
For Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Drag "Unplugged" into your Applications folder (optional, but tidy).
2. Double-click it.

YOUR MAC WILL WARN YOU THE FIRST TIME. HERE'S WHY, AND WHAT TO DO.
macOS will say it can't verify Unplugged ("Apple could not verify..." or "not opened").
  1. Click "Done" (or "OK"). Don't click "Move to Trash".
  2. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down. Next to "Unplugged was blocked...", click "Open Anyway".
  4. Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" once more.
You only have to do this once.

If your Mac instead says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", open the Terminal app, paste this
line, press Return, and then open Unplugged again:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Unplugged.app

This happens because Unplugged isn't notarized by Apple. That requires a $99/year Apple developer
membership, and Unplugged is a free project with nothing to sell. The warning means "Apple doesn't know
who made this", not "this is dangerous". If you'd like to check it yourself:
  - Every line of the code is public: github.com/Keep-AI-Local/unplugged
  - Each download's fingerprint (SHA-256) is listed on the GitHub release page.

Nothing is installed besides the app itself, and nothing you type ever leaves your Mac.
To remove Unplugged, drag it to the Trash.
