UNPLUGGED: AI that lives entirely on your computer · keepailocal.org

1. Drag "Unplugged" onto the "Applications" folder next to it.
2. Open Unplugged from your Applications folder (or Launchpad).

THE FIRST TIME, YOUR MAC WILL WARN YOU. HERE'S WHAT TO DO:
macOS says it can't verify Unplugged ("Apple could not verify..." / "not opened").
  1. Click "Done" (don't move it to the Trash).
  2. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down; next to "Unplugged was blocked...", click "Open Anyway".
  4. Enter your Mac password and click "Open Anyway" again.
Only needed once.

If instead it says the app "is damaged", open Terminal, paste this line and press Return:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Unplugged.app

Why: Unplugged isn't notarized by Apple (a $99/year membership), because it's a free project with
nothing to sell. The warning means "Apple doesn't know who made this", not "this is dangerous".
The code is public: github.com/Keep-AI-Local/unplugged
