UNPLUGGED: AI that lives entirely on your computer
Free · No account · No internet needed · keepailocal.org

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Extract the download (right-click "Unplugged-Linux-x64.tar.gz" -> "Extract Here").
2. Open the "Unplugged" folder and double-click "Unplugged".
   If nothing happens: right-click it -> "Run" / "Run as a Program", or open a terminal in the
   folder and type:   ./Unplugged

Uses your graphics card automatically if it supports Vulkan (most NVIDIA, AMD and Intel cards with
current drivers); otherwise it runs on your processor.

Nothing is installed, nothing is written to your computer, and nothing you type ever leaves it.
To remove Unplugged, just delete the folder.

Check it yourself: github.com/Keep-AI-Local/unplugged (fingerprints are on the release page).
